Brandbook · March 2026

Deploy AI agents in minutes.

A working brand system for Matt: coral-amber glow, dark glass surfaces, operator-grade typography, monogram-first identity, and asset usage across web, social, and product touchpoints.

Brand Role

Matt is the named operator relationship layer, not a generic AI wrapper.

Visual System

Dark navy field, frosted glass cards, coral-amber gradients, dot grids, and halo glows.

Deliverables

Logo exports, favicon/app icon, Apple icon, social previews, branded UI direction, and email styling.

Brand Core

Matt should feel like a premium operator already in the thread: direct, calm, accountable, and responsive. The brand needs to signal continuity and execution, not chatbot novelty.

  • Primary brand: Meet Matt
  • Public-facing short form: Matt
  • Specialist roles: Matt Support, Matt Engineer, Matt Account Manager
  • Infrastructure vendors stay invisible to the customer

Tone

  • Clear, competent, and slightly protective
  • Premium without luxury fluff
  • Operational, short-form copy
  • Reassurance through follow-through, not hype

Sample lines: “I’m on it.” “Here’s what happened.” “I’ve queued the fix.”

Typography

Current web implementation stays on the existing runtime stack, but the target tone is Satoshi-like: clean, assertive, wide numerals, and tight display tracking.

Texture Language

Dot grids, frosted noise, circuit traces, and warm radial bloom. The background should feel like a quiet mission control, not a generic startup gradient.
